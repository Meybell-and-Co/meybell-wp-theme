# Meybell Design Language

## Philosophy

Clarity before cleverness.

Editorial over promotional.

Museum signage over marketing brochure.

Human warmth over corporate polish.

---

## Color Roles

Gold
Purpose:
Attention, highlights, emphasis, action.

Do not use for:
Large backgrounds.

---

Charcoal

Purpose:
Primary text.

Represents confidence and permanence.

---

Cream

Purpose:
Canvas.

Whitespace should feel intentional.

---

## Typography

EB Garamond

Used for:
• Headlines
• Pull quotes
• Storytelling

Represents:
Tradition
Craft
Humanity

---

Montserrat

Used for:
• UI
• Body copy
• Navigation

Represents:
Clarity
Precision
Legibility
